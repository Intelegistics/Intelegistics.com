Photo slots: each spot on the site loads the file named below from this folder.

All photos are WebP, sized for the web (1400px wide for section photos, 1000px for the
What We Handle cards). That keeps the whole site's photos under 2 MB, and the site
preloads them in the background (assets/js/preload.js) so pages open with photos ready.

Adding or replacing a photo:
  1. Convert it to WebP at about 80% quality, no wider than the sizes above
     (squoosh.app does this in the browser).
  2. Save it here with the exact name below, ending in .webp, replacing the old file.
  A .jpg/.jpeg/.png with the right name still works if no .webp of that name exists,
  but it loads slower: delete the matching .webp first, or the old .webp keeps showing.

Browsers keep photos cached for up to an hour before checking for a new version, so
a replaced photo can take up to an hour to show for returning visitors (hard-refresh
to see it immediately).

Side-by-side section photos keep their own shape (no cropping). What We Handle cards
are shown at 4:3 and cropped to fill.
